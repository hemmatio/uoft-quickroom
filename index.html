ℹ️ About U of T QuickRoom
🏛️
U of T QuickRoom v1.0
Created by Omid Hemmati (omidhemmati.com)
A fast, multi-threaded LibCal space availability aggregator and reservation accelerator for University of Toronto students.
What is this project?

Booking a study room across U of T libraries normally requires opening dozens of LibCal tabs, clicking through dates and rooms one-by-one, and guessing when spaces are free. U of T QuickRoom unifies all 13 bookable libraries across St. George (UTSG), Mississauga (UTM), and Scarborough (UTSC) into a single instantaneous, parallel interface with 1-click booking.

How It Works
  • Concurrent Multi-Library Aggregator: Rather than querying libraries sequentially, QuickRoom's Python backend deploys a ThreadPoolExecutor that queries LibCal's availability grids across all 13 libraries and 170+ rooms simultaneously in milliseconds.
  • Contiguous Slot Interval Matching: LibCal exposes availability in 30-minute blocks. When you request a duration (e.g. 1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours), QuickRoom checks that every consecutive interval is completely vacant (s-lc-eq-avail) before considering the room available.
  • 1-Click Direct Booking Handoff: Clicking "Book Slot" passes the library ID, group ID, room ID, prefilled date, and verification checksum directly into U of T's official LibCal checkout portal.
  • UTSG Interactive Map Locator: Drag a rectangular bounding box over the stylized campus map to filter and focus solely on buildings within your preferred campus quad.
Group Size Policy & Rules

U of T libraries strictly regulate who can book which room, and violating policy leads to booking rejection by library staff:

  • Solo Study Spaces: Spaces like Robarts Stacks carrels, OISE individual carrels, and Graham Library single rooms have a policy restriction of exactly min_occupancy = 1 and max_occupancy = 1.
  • Group Study Spaces: Collaboration rooms in Gerstein, Robarts Common, and Pratt strictly mandate 2+, 3+, or 5+ people. Solo students are strictly prohibited from reserving these rooms.

How QuickRoom enforces policy: QuickRoom evaluates min_occupancy ≤ Group Size ≤ max_occupancy. Selecting "👤 1 Person (Individual only)" strictly filters out all group-only rooms so solo students never get disqualified, while choosing larger group sizes (2, 3, 4, 5, 6+) automatically filters out small carrels and displays only rooms that accommodate your group.
